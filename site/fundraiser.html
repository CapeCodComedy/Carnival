FUNDRAISE WITH THE CARNIVAL

50% GOES TO YOUR ORGANIZATION

Your organization gets its own checkout code. Anyone who buys a seat at 1140A.com and types it sends half the price of that seat to your organization — every seat in the house. Ticket prices stay the same for the buyer; the half comes out of our side.

THE MATH

One $66 seat → $33 to your organization
Ten seats → $330
Ten pairs → $660

No inventory, no cash handling, no quota, no cost. Your half is paid by check after the show, with the full order tally attached. The code has no limit and no exclusivity — send it to every family, member, and group chat you have, on-Cape or off.
