    ABOUT THE LANGUAGE: PLAINLY, BEFORE YOU BUY

    This is grown-up stand-up. Expect adult language and adult themes, roughly what late-night network television lets through, and nothing you wouldn't overhear on a busy street. It is not explicit-record territory: nothing risqué for its own sake, nobody in the crowd gets attacked. An evening ticketed show is an adult room by nature; we say this so no one is surprised, not to scare anyone off.

    *A NOTE ON THE $3, BECAUSE WE'D RATHER BE UPFRONT

    The $3 per ticket is a card-processing fee charged by our payment processor, not by us. The 1140A Corporation receives none of it. It goes straight to the processor to cover the cost of taking your card safely. We pass it through rather than hiding it inside a higher ticket price. Your ticket price is the ticket price.
