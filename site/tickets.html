    Wheelchair seating — the short terms

    Three terms, in plain English. When the end of the terms is on your screen, the agree button wakes.

    What you're booking

    A dedicated wheelchair space on the Tier 2 cross-aisle (row K), sized for a wheelchair or mobility device, with clear sightlines and an aisle-side approach. Each space can pair with one adjacent companion seat. Both price at the ordinary Tier 2 rate with the card fee waived — access never costs extra here.

    The terms

    1. Wheelchair spaces exist for patrons who attend in a wheelchair or mobility device. Booking one is your statement that it's for such a patron in your party. Nobody will ever ask you for proof of disability — not at checkout, not at the door.

    2. The space itself is the seat: it fits a wheelchair, and it is where the wheelchair parks for the show.

    3. A ticket obtained by misrepresentation is void and non-refundable — the wheelchair space and its paired companion seat are both forfeit (just the single seat, if booked alone). There is nothing to chase and nothing to dispute: the ticket is simply void, on terms you read right here.

    Parking, while we have you

    ALL VEHICLES ENTERING THE SPECIAL ACCESS PARKING LOT WILL BE CHECKED FOR A VALID DISABILITY PARKING PLACARD OR PLATE. YOU MAY BE ASKED TO SHOW YOUR TICKET.

    A kind request, not a demand: display your placard or plate where the volunteer can spot it. "May be asked" means exactly that.
