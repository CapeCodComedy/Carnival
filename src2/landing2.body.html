THE COMICS

BRENDAN SAGALOW

HEADLINER · NATIONALLY TOURING

Equal parts confident social observation and cutting self-deprecation — he has owned New York stages for over 10 years. His debut hour, Thin Lips (YouTube, 2024), keeps collecting critical acclaim; a “New Face” of the 2022 Just For Laughs festival, with runs on Barstool's Answer the Internet and MTV's Joking Off.

MIKE CANNON

HEADLINER · NATIONALLY TOURING

He has headlined some of the world's most iconic rooms, including a sold-out Gramercy Theatre. His special Traumatized Animal (2024) drew millions of views across social media, and his first album, I Think It Just Kicked In, hit #1 on the iTunes comedy chart.

JASON CHOI

OPENING THE NIGHT

Named New York's Funniest for the New York Comedy Festival 2025, with his network TV debut on CBS's Comics Unleashed in 2026. A finalist at the Boston Comedy Festival and the New York Comedy Club Fall Invitational, he has opened for Ronny Chieng, Mike Vecchione, and Gary Gulman.

WHY THIS EXISTS

The 1140A Corporation is a Cape Cod nonprofit, run entirely by volunteers, with operating costs covered by donations — so the money goes to the artists and to keeping ticket prices within reach. The mission: bring top touring comedy back to the Cape at prices locals can afford, the way the Cape had it in the '80s and '90s and lost. This night is the first of a planned series.
